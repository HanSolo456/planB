# Aviation Regulations, Passenger Rights & Travel Cancellation Policies

## 1. Aviation Regulatory Framework (DGCA India Passenger Charter)
In Indian civil aviation (governed by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation - DGCA CAR Section 3, Series M, Part IV), passengers possess statutory protections when commercial flights undergo delays, cancellations, or denied boarding.

### 1.1 Flight Delays
When an airline expects a flight delay beyond the scheduled time of departure:
- **Delay of 2 Hours or more** (for flights with block time up to 2.5 hours): The airline must provide free meals and refreshments.
- **Delay of 3 Hours or more** (for flights with block time between 2.5 and 5 hours): The airline must provide free meals and refreshments.
- **Delay of 4 Hours or more** (for flights not covered above): Free meals and refreshments.
- **Delay of 6 Hours or more** (announced 24 hours prior): Airline must offer an alternate flight or provide a full refund of ticket value.
- **Delay between 20:00 and 03:00 exceeding 6 hours**, or any delay exceeding 24 hours: The airline must provide free hotel accommodation, including airport transfers.
- **Exceptions (Force Majeure)**: Compensation is exempt if delays are caused by extraordinary circumstances (adverse meteorological conditions, air traffic control congestion, security threats, political instability). However, meals and refreshments remain mandatory regardless of cause.

### 1.2 Flight Cancellations
- **Notice > 2 Weeks prior**: Airline must offer an alternate flight or full refund.
- **Notice between 24 Hours and 2 Weeks**: Airline must offer an alternate flight scheduled within 2 hours of original departure or full refund.
- **Notice < 24 Hours or at Airport**:
  - In addition to a full refund or alternate flight, airline must pay financial compensation:
    - Block time up to 1 hr: ₹5,000 or one-way basic fare + fuel charge (whichever is lower).
    - Block time > 1 hr and up to 2 hrs: ₹7,500 or one-way basic fare + fuel charge.
    - Block time > 2 hrs: ₹10,000 or one-way basic fare + fuel charge.

### 1.3 Connecting Flights & PNR Protection
- **Single PNR Bookings**: If a delay on Leg 1 causes a missed connection on Leg 2 under a single ticket, the operating carrier is legally obligated to reroute the passenger to the final destination at no additional charge, provide care (meals, hotel), or issue a full refund for the untraveled segments.
- **Self-Transfer / Split PNR Bookings**: If flights are purchased on separate tickets/PNRs, airlines treat them as unrelated contracts. If Leg 1 is delayed, Leg 2 is classified as a passenger "No-Show" with 100% loss of fare. Minimum safe self-transfer buffer at Indian metro airports is 180 minutes domestic-to-domestic, and 240 minutes international-to-domestic.

## 2. Airport Specific Operational Dynamics

### 2.1 Delhi Indira Gandhi International Airport (DEL)
- **Terminals**:
  - Terminal 1 (T1): Domestic low-cost carriers (IndiGo, SpiceJet).
  - Terminal 2 (T2): Domestic low-cost carriers.
  - Terminal 3 (T3): All international flights, full-service domestic carriers (Air India, Vistara), and select IndiGo domestic flights.
- **Inter-Terminal Transit**:
  - Distance between T1 and T2/T3 is ~8 km. Inter-terminal airport shuttle or Delhi Metro requires minimum **90 to 120 minutes** transfer buffer.
- **Winter Fog / Smog Disruptions**: Between December and January, CAT III-B Instrument Landing System (ILS) conditions regularly trigger low-visibility ground delay programs (GDP) causing 60-180 minute cascading ground delays.

### 2.2 Goa Airports: Dabolim (GOI) vs Manohar International Airport Mopa (GOX)
- **Goa has two operational international airports**:
  - **Dabolim (GOI)**: Located in South Goa. Average taxi travel time to North Goa (Candolim, Calangute, Vagator) is 60–90 minutes.
  - **Manohar Mopa (GOX)**: Located in North Goa border (Pernem). Average taxi travel time to North Goa (Vagator, Anjuna, Morjim) is 35–50 minutes; travel time to South Goa (Benaulim, Palolem) is 100–140 minutes.
- **Transfer Impact**: Mistaking GOI for GOX in ground transport bookings creates an immediate 40–70 minute schedule error, directly risking check-in and scheduled excursions.

## 3. Accommodation & Activity Cancellation Rules

### 3.1 Hotel Cancellation & No-Show Mechanics
- **Standard Cancellation Windows**:
  - **Flexible Rate**: Free cancellation up to 24–48 hours prior to check-in (14:00 or 15:00 local time).
  - **Semi-Flexible**: 50% refund if cancelled within 24 hours of arrival; 100% refund if cancelled before.
  - **Non-Refundable**: 0% refund once booked.
- **The "No-Show" Risk**: If a guest fails to check in by 23:59 on Day 1 without notifying the front desk, hotel management systems automatically mark the reservation as a "No-Show". This voids all remaining nights in multi-day stays without refund.
- **Automated Mitigation Action**: When a flight disruption delays arrival past 21:00, the system must issue an automated "Late Check-in Guarantee Notice" to the hotel with the updated estimated time of arrival (ETA).

### 3.2 Tour & Activity Cancellation Policies
- **Adventure & Water Activities (e.g., Scuba Diving, Paragliding, Boat Charters)**:
  - Subject to strict 24-hour non-refundable cutoffs due to instructor and boat scheduling.
  - **Disruption Recovery Protocol**: Most operators allow a "Date/Time Slide" (postponing to next day morning) with zero penalty if notified at least 4 hours in advance, whereas cancellations within 2 hours result in 100% loss.
